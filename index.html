Comment themes

What the comments are about

The audit server reads every free-text answer (call synopsis, the "why" answers and the issue comments) and gives each one a theme and a segment, using built-in keyword rules in English, Hindi and Hinglish. It runs on our own server: nothing is sent outside and there is no cost. Categories are saved, so a later run only looks at new or edited comments.

Lead pool

The leads auditors can look up, built from your Excel files, of any size. Choose the files that changed and click Upload and build. The server reads them, matches leads to Lead Data and Calling Data, masks phone numbers and Lead IDs, and replaces the pool for everyone. A file you don't choose again keeps its last upload. Saved audits are not changed.
